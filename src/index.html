    Valve settings

    How the 1949 machine computes

    Every tank is a stock of money and every pipe a flow per quarter. The pump lifts the transactions balances to the top as national income Y. Slotted valves split it: taxes tY to the Treasury, saving s(Y−T) to surplus balances, imports m·C to foreign-held balances. The rest is domestic consumption, which falls back with government spending, investment and exports to refill the transactions tank.

    Phillips's float trick is kept: the water level in the surplus-balances tank sets the interest rate (more idle money, lower r), and r throttles the investment valve. With a floating rate, foreign balances move the exchange rate, which moves exports and imports.

    Equations

    • dTB/dt = C − Im + I + G + X − Y; output follows spending power through the production lag, dY/dt = (TB − Y)/τp
    • dSB/dt = S − I + open-market flow − deficit borrowing
    • r = 12 − 0.14·SB (0.5 to 15%), I = 12 + b(5 − r) + a·dY/dt
    • e = 1 + 0.6(FB − 40)/40, X = X₀·e^0.9, import share m/e^0.6
    • Unemployment u = 1.5 + 30(1 − Y/105)%, an illustrative link to the 1958 curve
    • Governor: demand D = Kp·gap + Ki·∫gap + Kd·d(gap)/dt, actual dΔG/dt = (D − ΔG)/τ, spending G = G₀ + ΔG

    Coefficients are chosen so a 1949-ish steady state near Y≈100 exists; they are not Phillips's UK calibration.

    What changed since 1949

    The central bank no longer rations a stock of money. It sets the cash rate to steer inflation toward 2–3%, using a rule that responds to underlying (trimmed-mean) inflation and the output gap, so it can look through temporary energy spikes in headline CPI, and it moves in 25-point steps at eight meetings a year. That is the RBA box at the top of the board.

    Most money is now created by commercial banks when they lend. New mortgage credit is a tap that adds water to the economy; repayments drain it away. Mortgage interest is the Australian cash-flow channel: higher rates pull money out of borrowers' spending almost immediately.

    Deficits are financed by selling bonds out of savings, and the RBA can buy bonds (QE) to inject money when the cash rate hits its floor. Capital flows chase the gap between Australian and US rates and move the dollar, which moves exports, imports and imported inflation.

    This is a stylised teaching model calibrated to those figures, not a forecast. Money moves only through pipes: an empty tank throttles its outlets, and only bank lending, repayment and QE change the total, which the test-suite checks at every step. It has no trend growth, so the public debt ratio uses the textbook erosion term (2% + inflation)·debt.

    Key equations

    • RBA rule on underlying inflation: i* = 0.6 + π + φπ(π − 2.5) + φy·gap, moved 25bp per meeting

    • Expectations learn from headline CPI and are pulled toward the target
    • Credit: L = credit·L̄(1 − 0.10(i − πᵉ − 0.6)), repayments D/75; mortgage interest (i + spread)·D
    • Capital inflow ∝ (i − i_US); AUD follows foreign-held balances and commodity prices
    • Unemployment u = 4.5 − 0.5·gap (Okun)
